Overview

Atmos Energy gas service: start, connection, transfer, and disconnect explained

Atmos Energy gas service is often requested when a homeowner, renter, landlord, or business needs natural gas for heating, cooking, hot water, and appliances. The process can vary by property type, meter availability, and whether a gas line is already connected or needs a new service order. In many cases, service start requests require address verification, eligibility checks, a scheduled connection date, and possible inspection before activation.

Start service

How to start Atmos gas service

  1. Confirm your service address. Make sure the property address is complete and matches the account or meter location.
  2. Select your move-in or start date. The requested date can affect service readiness and scheduling.
  3. Complete verification. Utility providers may need identity, occupancy, or account details before the service request is approved.
  4. Check meter and service availability. Some homes require a new meter, service line connection, or inspection before gas can be turned on.
  5. Schedule activation or inspection. If required, this can add time before the gas is active.
  6. Confirm gas is active. Once approved, service should be activated based on the schedule and any safety checks that were completed.

Same-day & urgent service

Can I get same-day Atmos gas service or immediate activation?

Same-day or immediate gas service is not always available. It depends on the address, whether a meter already exists, whether the line is ready, whether an inspection is needed, and whether the service request can be processed before the local work window closes. Many customers use the phone number for urgent verification when gas is needed on move-in day or when service is delayed.

If you need natural gas on a specific date, it is usually best to request service as early as possible, verify the service address, and ask about same-day options if your move is urgent.

Common reasons gas is not active on move-in day

  • Address not yet recognized in the service database
  • Meter not installed or not ready for activation
  • Verification or identity check still pending
  • Safety inspection required before gas can be turned on
  • Previous account or disconnect issue still on record
Stop service

How to stop or disconnect Atmos gas service

Customers may need to stop gas service when moving out of a property, vacating a rental, or closing an account. The service disconnect process typically involves selecting a final service date, confirming final meter read or account information, and ensuring the request is processed before you move.

  1. Request the disconnect date
  2. Confirm the property address and account details
  3. Check whether final bill or account closure is required
  4. Ask if a service inspection or final read is needed

Typical issues when stopping service

  • Timing mismatch between move-out and disconnect date
  • Service still active after the tenant leaves
  • Final bill remains open because of pending usage
  • Address change or account transfer confusion
Transfer service

How to transfer Atmos Energy gas service to a new address

Service transfer usually happens when a customer is moving to a new residence or location that is already in the Atmos service territory. In many cases, the request must include the old address, the new address, the effective date, and account verification details.

  1. Provide the old address and existing service account information
  2. Enter the new service address
  3. Select the transfer date
  4. Confirm any required inspection, meter, or service setup at the new location
  5. Verify that service is active at move-in

Important transfer checks

  • Is the new address eligible for Atmos gas service?
  • Does the property already have a gas meter?
  • Is there an existing account still active?
  • Are there scheduling or inspection requirements at the new address?
Safety & inspections

Why some Atmos gas service requests require inspection

Gas service activation often includes safety requirements. A service request may require a meter check, line inspection, or safety testing before natural gas can be turned on. This helps keep the system compliant and reduces the risk of unsafe or improperly connected equipment. If the property is new, recently renovated, or has not previously had gas service, an inspection may be more likely.

  • New gas meter installation or replacement may be needed
  • Line or meter safety inspection may be required
  • Account verification may need to be completed before activation
  • Utility scheduling could delay same-day service requests
Common problems

Why Atmos gas service may be delayed or not active

  • Service request submitted but no activation: possible missing verification or incomplete scheduling
  • No gas meter installed: a new connection may be necessary before activation
  • Address not recognized: an exact property address may be required
  • Prior account still active: older services or closed accounts can cause confusion
  • Inspection pending: safety review may delay activation
  • Move-in date passed: urgent validation may be necessary
Important: Activation dates and availability vary by location, service type, and utility scheduling. For urgent move-in or gas outage situations, it is smart to contact the service line directly for confirmation.

If gas is not active when expected, verify the move-in date, account status, meter readiness, and whether a scheduling or inspection step is still pending.

FAQ

People also ask about Atmos gas service start, stop, and transfer

How do I start Atmos gas service?

Enter your address, select the start or move-in date, complete verification, and schedule any needed meter or inspection step.

Can I start gas service by phone?

Yes. In urgent or time-sensitive situations, phone support can help confirm service status or next steps.

How long does Atmos gas service take to start?

Most new gas service requests are completed in 1–3 business days, but delays can happen with inspections, meter setup, or schedule constraints.

Does Atmos Energy require an inspection before gas activation?

Sometimes. In many cases, safety inspections or meter checks are needed before service is activated.

How do I stop Atmos gas service?

Request a disconnect at the desired date, confirm final account details, and make sure the request is processed before move-out.

How do I transfer Atmos gas service?

Provide the existing account info and new service address, then schedule the transfer date and confirm any service setup needed at the new property.

What if gas is not active after move-in?

Check if verification is pending, the meter is ready, or inspection has not been completed. Delays can often be resolved by confirming the status quickly.

Can I get same-day Atmos gas service?

Same-day service may be available on a limited basis if the address, meter, and inspection conditions allow it.
